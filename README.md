# CAST

## **C**oncept **A**rt **S**tarter **T**oolkit

> **CAST** helps you decide *what to draw*. It uses a systematic approach, based on
> the components of storytelling, to build the foundation of a drawing.
> CAST describes it. You draw it.

---

## The pitch

*September 30, 2026*

When I'm learning to draw, the hardest part is often deciding what to draw. I end up
drawing the same kinds of things over and over.

What if a tool helped me create what I draw, not by drawing it, but by describing it?
A foundation to channel my imagination and start from.

---

## The system

You build what you'll draw in **layers**. First you choose a subject. Then each layer
answers one question about it, and each layer shapes the ones after it: the story and
the world explain the look, and the personality explains the pose.

```
Subject ─▶ Identity ─▶ Story ─▶ World ─▶ Body / Build ─▶ Look ─▶ Moment
```

| Layer               | Question                    | Being                            | Vehicle                                                |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. Subject**      | What will I draw?           | Being                            | Vehicle                                                |
| **2. Identity**     | What is it?                 | Type, Age, Gender                | Type, Size                                             |
| **3. Story**        | Who is it?                  | Backstory, Goal and flaw, Twist  | Backstory, Mission and flaw, Twist                     |
| **4. World**        | Where and when?             | Time period, Place               | Time period, Place                                     |
| **5. Body / Build** | How is it built?            | Shape language, Body, Body parts | Shape language, Terrain, Power source, Crew, Operation |
| **6. Look**         | How does it present itself? | Wardrobe, Accessories            | Livery, Accessories                                    |
| **7. Moment**       | What's happening right now? | Pose, Facial expression          | *To define*                                            |

Some steps are **shaped by** earlier choices. Their options are filtered or suggested
by what you picked before.

## 2. Identity

*What is it?*

### Being

#### Type

- **Human**
- **Alien**
- **Robot**
- **Fantastic creature**
- **Combination**: a mix of two or more of the options above

#### Age

The age of the being, as a **stage**, not a number:

- **Baby**
- **Child**
- **Young**
- **Adult**
- **Elder**
- **Ancient**

#### Gender

- **Female**
- **Male**
- **Neutral**

### Vehicle

#### Type

- **Mechanical**
- **Biomechanical**

#### Size

- **Small**
- **Medium**
- **Large**
- **Gigantic**

---

## 3. Story

*Who is it?*

### Backstory

A little history of the subject:

- **Being**: its **Profession** and **Personality**
- **Vehicle**: its own history, like a retired warship or a smuggler's ship

### Goal and flaw

What the subject wants, and what gets in the way. Both show up visually: a character
afraid of the cold wears too many layers, and one who wants to be taken seriously wears
an oversized hat.

- **Being**
  - **Goal**: what the character wants
  - **Flaw or fear**: what holds them back
- **Vehicle**
  - **Mission**: what the vehicle is for
  - **Flaw**: what holds it back

### Twist

One deliberate contradiction that breaks the cliché, so I don't end up drawing the same
things over and over. For example, a gentle giant, an ancient being who acts like a child,
or a luxury yacht used as a warship.

## 4. World

*Where and when?*

### Time period

More than past, present, and future: specific time periods, for example:

- **Victorian Era**
- **Ancient Egypt**
- **Ancient Rome**
- **Age of Piracy**

### Place

Where the subject is.

## 5. Body / Build

*How is it built?*

### Shape language

The basic shape the design is built from. Each shape carries a meaning:

- **Circle**: friendly, soft, approachable
- **Square**: sturdy, reliable, stubborn
- **Triangle**: dangerous, dynamic, sharp

*Shaped by:* Backstory, Personality, and Twist. It works for vehicles too: a round,
friendly submarine versus an angular warship.

### Being

#### Body

The general basis of the body.

- **Body type**: **Ectomorph**, **Mesomorph**, **Endomorph**
- **Stature**: **Short**, **Average**, **Tall**

#### Body parts

Each part of the body, divided into three main categories.
*Shaped by:* Type. For example, tentacles for an alien, wheels for a robot.

- **Head**
  - **Hair**: type of hair, or no hair
  - **Scars**
- **Upper body**: the number and type of limbs
  - **Arms**
  - **Tentacles**
  - **Wings**
  - **Mechanical arms**
  - **Claws or pincers**
- **Lower body**: the number and type of limbs
  - **Legs**
  - **Animal legs**
  - **Tail**
  - **Tentacles**
  - **Wheels or treads**
  - **Hovering** (no lower limbs)

### Vehicle

#### Terrain

The type of terrain it can travel on:

- **Land**
- **Water**
- **Air**
- **Space**
- **Combination**: a mix of two or more of the options above

#### Power source

- **Fuel**
- **Electric**
- **Solar**
- **Nuclear**
- **Magic**

#### Crew

- **Solo**
- **Small crew**
- **Large crew**

#### Operation

How the vehicle is operated.

- **Movement**: the parts that make it move, for example **number of wheels**,
  **type of wings** if it's a plane, or **rotors**.
  *Shaped by:* Terrain.
- **Steering**: how to steer it.

---

## 6. Look

*How does it present itself?*

### Wardrobe / Livery

How the subject looks. Closely related to the place.
*Shaped by:* Time period, Place, Backstory, and Goal and flaw.

- **Being — Wardrobe**: what the character wears
- **Vehicle — Livery**: its paint, decals, or armor plating

### Accessories

Any sort of accessory, depending on the previous categories.
*Shaped by:* everything before it, especially Backstory and Place.
